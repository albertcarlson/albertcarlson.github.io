# Map data

`world-map.svg` is derived from Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons.

- Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
- Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
- Public-domain terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Coordinates are transformed to the game's 900 × 440 equirectangular view, longitude −180…180 and latitude 85…−85. Geometry outside this latitude range is clipped by the viewport. No remote map requests occur during gameplay.
