How to play

Click a parked plane or its departure button to take off. Choose its next destination before departure. Tickets are paid only on landing: passengers × ticket price.

Passenger demand depends on the departure airport, varies randomly, and never exceeds your seat capacity. Buy airports in order to open new routes. New planes start in Berlin.

Fleet upgrades apply to every plane on its next departure. A manager automatically launches landed planes after 10, 9, ..., 3, 2, then 1 second. Each automatic departure randomly chooses an unlocked airport other than the current one. The destination selector applies to manual departures.

Airport services earn cash every second without flying. Your airline saves locally and runs for up to 8 hours while away; only a manager can start new flights while you’re gone.
